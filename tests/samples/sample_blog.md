# Coffee Grinder Guide for Beginners

A coffee grinder is the easiest way to make better coffee at home. Fresh grounds keep more flavor than pre-ground coffee from a bag.

![A burr coffee grinder on a kitchen counter](https://example.com/images/coffee-grinder.jpg)

## Why a Coffee Grinder Matters

Coffee starts to lose its aroma soon after it is ground. When you grind just before brewing, your cup tastes brighter. Studies say 80% of flavor is lost in the first hour.

## Burr or Blade?

A burr grinder crushes beans between two plates, so every piece is about the same size. A blade grinder chops the beans like a blender. The pieces come out uneven. Uneven pieces brew unevenly. For more, [click here](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coffee_preparation).

#### Picking a grind size

Match the grind size to your brewer. Use coarse for French press, medium for drip, and fine for espresso.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is a coffee grinder worth it for beginners?

Yes. Even a cheap burr grinder makes a big difference in taste.

### How often should I clean my grinder?

Brush it out once a week and deep clean it once a month.
