Create an outline for an SEO blog post.

Topic: {topic}
Target audience: {audience}
Tone: {tone}
Goal of the blog: {goal}
Focus keyword: {focus_keyword}
Secondary keywords: {secondary_keywords}
Questions real people ask about this topic:
{faq_questions}

Rules for the outline:
1. "h1": a clear headline that contains the exact phrase "{focus_keyword}".
2. "intro_notes": what the intro should say. The intro must use "{focus_keyword}" in its first two sentences.
3. "sections": 4 to 6 H2 sections in a logical order for this audience.
   - At least one H2 must contain the exact phrase "{focus_keyword}".
   - Add H3 sub-points ("h3s") only where they genuinely help.
   - In "notes", say what to cover and which secondary keyword fits there.
   - The last section may be a short wrap-up. Do NOT include an FAQ section here.
4. "faq_questions": choose {faq_count} questions from the list above (light rewording is fine).
   Each must end with a question mark.
